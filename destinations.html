Canyon
Charyn Canyon

Discover dramatic red cliffs and the famous Valley of Castles in southeastern Kazakhstan.

Best for: Hiking, photography and exploring unusual rock formations.

Tip: Bring water and comfortable walking shoes, especially in warm weather.

Lake
Kolsai Lakes

Enjoy beautiful alpine lakes surrounded by mountain forests and impressive peaks.

Best for: Hiking, nature photography and mountain scenery.

Tip: Check trail conditions and weather before travelling.

Lake
Kaindy Lake

Visit a remarkable mountain lake known for its turquoise water and submerged tree trunks.

Best for: Photography and discovering unusual natural landscapes.

Tip: The road can be rough, so check transport options before your trip.

National Park
Burabay

Relax among pine forests, lakes and distinctive rock formations in northern Kazakhstan.

Best for: Relaxing, walking, boating and family trips.

Tip: Plan extra time for viewpoints and lakeside walks.

National Park
Altyn-Emel

Explore desert landscapes, distinctive dunes and the diverse wildlife of a large national park.

Best for: Nature exploration, landscapes and wildlife watching.

Tip: Arrange suitable transport and check park access requirements in advance.

Lake
Lake Alakol

Enjoy a lakeside escape with wide-open views and popular summer recreation areas.

Best for: Summer holidays, lakeside relaxation and photography.

Tip: Check local weather and accommodation availability before visiting.
