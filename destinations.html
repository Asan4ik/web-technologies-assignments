Top Destinations

Explore the most popular places across Kazakhstan.
From majestic mountains to modern cities — find your next adventure.

Almaty

A vibrant city at the foot of the Zailiysky Alatau mountains. Perfect for nature lovers and city explorers.

Astana

Modern architecture, cultural attractions and the heart of modern Kazakhstan.

Burabay

Crystal clear lakes, pine forests and beautiful landscapes. A perfect place for relaxation.

Charyn Canyon

One of Kazakhstan's most famous natural wonders with breathtaking rock formations.

Kolsai Lakes

A series of stunning mountain lakes surrounded by forests. A paradise for hikers and nature lovers.

Altyn-Emel

Home to the famous Singing Dune and unique natural landscapes.

Bozzhyra

A scenic area known for its natural beauty and tranquility.

Alakol

A beautiful lake surrounded by mountains and forests.

Barsakelmes

A unique natural area with diverse ecosystems and wildlife.
