Trip guide

Everything you need to know to make your next trip to Kazakhstan unforgettable.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Kazakhstan is during the spring and summer months, from May to September. The weather is warm and pleasant, making it ideal for outdoor activities and sightseeing.

Travel Costs

Travel costs in Kazakhstan can vary depending on your travel style and preferences. On average, a budget traveler can expect to spend around $30-$50 per day, while mid-range travelers may spend around $50-$100 per day. Luxury travelers can expect to spend upwards of $150 per day.

Category Budget Mid-range Comfort

Transportation
By Train

The train network in Kazakhstan is well-developed and offers a scenic way to travel between major cities.

By Air

Main cities are connected by domestic flights, making air travel a convenient option for longer distances.

By Bus

Buses are a common mode of transportation for shorter distances and are widely available throughout the country.

By Car

Driving in Kazakhstan can be an adventure, with many scenic routes and well-maintained highways connecting major cities.

Travel Tips
  • Learn some basic Russian or Kazakh phrases to help with communication.
  • Carry cash, as not all places accept credit cards.
  • Respect local customs and traditions.
  • Stay hydrated and protect yourself from the sun during the summer months.
  • Check the weather forecast and pack accordingly.
